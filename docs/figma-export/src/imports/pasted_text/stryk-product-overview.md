# STRYK

## Mission

STRYK exists to give amateur football players a persistent digital football identity.

Millions of players participate in local football matches every week, but none of their performances, achievements, reputation, or history are recorded in a meaningful way.

Professional footballers have statistics, ratings, trophies, scouting reports, career history, and recognition.

Amateur players have WhatsApp groups.

STRYK bridges that gap.

The platform transforms casual local football into a structured football journey where every match, performance, achievement, and contribution becomes part of a player's long-term football identity.

---

# Core Product Philosophy

The primary asset inside STRYK is not the match.

The primary asset is the player.

Everything revolves around helping a player build:

- Identity
- Reputation
- History
- Recognition
- Community

The platform should feel closer to a football game career mode than a sports utility application.

The user should feel:

"This is my football career."

not

"This is another booking app."

# Core Problem

Current football communities suffer from several issues:

## No Identity

Players have no persistent football profile.

There is no place where someone can showcase:

- Position
- Style
- Achievements
- Match history

---

## No Verified Statistics

Goals and assists are forgotten.

Performances disappear after every game.

Players rely on memory and opinion.

---

## No Reputation Layer

Reliable players and unreliable players appear equal.

There is no system that rewards:

- Attendance
- Honesty
- Consistency
- Performance

---

## No Football History

A player may participate in hundreds of matches and have no historical record.

Their football journey effectively disappears.

---

# Solution

STRYK creates a football identity layer.

Every player receives:

- A profile
- A football card
- A reputation score
- Match history
- Verified statistics
- Achievements
- Social connections

The platform continuously evolves this identity through participation.

# Product Pillars

## Pillar 1 — Identity

Every player owns a football profile.

Identity includes:

- Name
- Username
- Avatar
- Preferred Position
- Secondary Position
- Preferred Foot
- Play Style
- Bio

This profile becomes the foundation of everything else.

---

## Pillar 2 — Player Cards

Player cards are the centerpiece of STRYK.

Inspired by:

- EA FC Ultimate Team
- eFootball
- Sports trading cards

A card represents a player's football identity.

Cards contain:

- Overall Rating
- Position
- Play Style
- Key Attributes
- Reputation
- Match Count
- Achievements

Cards are dynamic.

As players participate and improve, cards evolve.

---

## Pillar 3 — Verified Match Records

Every match becomes a structured record.

A match includes:

- Venue
- Date
- Teams
- Participants
- Score
- Events
- Notes

The goal is to transform a temporary game into a permanent record.

---

## Pillar 4 — Community Verification

STRYK must not rely solely on self-reported statistics.

Instead:

Players submit their performance.

Other participants validate those claims.

Examples:

Goals
Assists
Saves
Tackles
Interceptions

Claims only become permanent when sufficient verification is received.

This creates trust.

---

## Pillar 5 — Reputation

Reputation is more valuable than raw statistics.

A player earns reputation through:

- Participation
- Consistency
- Verification accuracy
- Attendance
- Community trust

Reputation should influence how other players perceive them.

---

## Pillar 6 — Social Graph

Players build football relationships.

Users can:

- Add friends
- Follow careers
- Join matches together
- Compare cards
- View achievements

Over time STRYK becomes a football network.
